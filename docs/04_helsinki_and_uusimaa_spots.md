## English Version

## 📍 Top Public-Transit Spots in the Helsinki Capital Region

        
One of the finest aspects of living in Helsinki is that exceptional sea and river fishing is directly reachable by HSL buses, trams, and metro. You do not need a car or boat to catch dinner.

        
          
| Location | Public Transit (HSL) | Target Species | Permits Required | Best Tactics & Key Spots |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Vanhankaupunginkoski** (Quiet Pool / Suvanto) | Bus 55, 71, 78, 506 to *Tekniikan museo* | Zander, Perch, Pike, Bream | Kalastonhoitomaksu (or general free rights for hook & line) | The calm tidal pool below the rapids. Jigging from the wooden walkways in summer evenings for zander. |
| **Vanhankaupunginkoski** (Upper Rapids / Koski) | Same as above | Sea Trout, Salmon, Stocked Rainbow Trout, Whitefish | **Special Rapid Permit** (Helsinki City Permit) | Fly fishing and spin-casting in roaring white water. Mandatory barbless single hooks and strict catch quotas. |
| **Lauttasaari Bridges & Shores** | Metro to *Lauttasaari* or Bus 21 / 22 | Baltic Herring, Sea Trout, Perch | 100% Free for Herring litka; Kalastonhoitomaksu for spin-casting | *Lauttasaarensilta* for spring herring runs. Rocky southern tip (Särkiniemi) for autumn sea trout spin-casting. |
| **Ruoholahti Canal & Jätkäsaari** | Metro to *Ruoholahti*, Tram 8 / 9 | Perch, Zander | Kalastonhoitomaksu | Urban "street fishing" after dark. Jigging with dropshot and small softbaits along the illuminated concrete quay walls. |
| **Seurasaari & Meilahti Headlands** | Bus 24 to *Seurasaari* | Perch, Pike, Sea Trout | Kalastonhoitomaksu | Rocky shorelines and points facing open sea. Cast medium Kuusamo spoons for pike around rocky drop-offs. |
| **Uutela & Kallahdenniemi** (Vuosaari) | Metro to *Vuosaari* + Bus 560 | Whitefish, Sea Trout, Perch, Pike | Kalastonhoitomaksu (or general free rights for bottom angling) | Shallow sandbars at Kallahdenniemi for early spring *siianonginta*; scenic pine headlands at Uutela for autumn sea trout. |
| **Töölönlahti** (City Center Basin) | Walking distance from Central Railway Station | Pike, Perch | Kalastonhoitomaksu | Recreational urban casting along park shores. Heavily weeded in late summer; use weedless spoons. |

        

        
## 🌲 Greater Uusimaa & Flowing River Waters

        
          - **Vantaanjoki Rapids (Pitkäkoski & Ruutinkoski)**: Located on the Helsinki–Vantaa border, easily reached by bus. Excellent flowing stretches for brown trout, grayling, and rainbow trout. Requires the Vantaanjoki joint permit (*Vantaanjoen yhteislupa*).
          - **Espoonlahti (Espoo Bay)**: Deep brackish fjord with extensive reed beds. Famous for trophy zander in early June and monster pike in October.
          - **Porkkalanniemi (Kirkkonummi)**: Dramatic granite cliffs jutting into the open Gulf of Finland. Premier spring and autumn venue for casting long spoons into the surf for migrating sea trout.
        

        
## 🗺️ Beyond the Capital: Iconic Finnish Fishing Regions

        
          - **Lake Saimaa (*Saimaa*)**: Finland's largest lake and the fourth largest in Europe. A maze of 14,000 islands teeming with giant pike, zander, perch, and landlocked salmonids. Perfect for cottage rental holidays.
          - **River Tornionjoki (Lapland)**: Europe's largest free-flowing, undammed salmon river on the Swedish border. Legendary for wild salmon up to 15–20 kg caught from traditional wooden rowing boats (*jokivene*).
          - **Lake Inari (*Inarijärvi*)**: The arctic sea of Lapland. Crystal-clear waters holding lake trout, arctic char, and giant grayling amidst total subarctic wilderness.

## 🌲 大乌西玛区域（大赫尔辛基拓展水系）

        
          - **万塔河激流群（Pitkäkoski 与 Ruutinkoski）**：位于赫尔辛基与万塔交界的大片天然森林保护区内，公交直达。水质清冽，春季与秋季盛产野生褐鳟、茴鱼与放流虹鳟，需购买万塔河联合急流证（*Vantaanjoen yhteislupa*）。
          - **Espoonlahti（埃斯波湾）**：典型的内陆延伸型微咸海湾，两侧密布茂密芦苇荡，是6月初初夏夜晚路亚大梭鲈和10月深秋捕猎巨型米级狗鱼的温床。
          - **Porkkalanniemi（波尔卡拉角）**：突入芬兰湾深处的壮丽花岗岩半岛。春秋两季大风浪天，无数路亚老手身穿防浪服在此面朝怒海，向浪花中大力抛投细长亮片搜索洄游海鳟。
        

        
## 🗺️ 走出首都：芬兰三大传世名胜水系

        
          - **塞马湖（Lake Saimaa）**：芬兰第一大湖，欧洲第四大湖。由14,000座岛屿构成的迷宫般水系，盛产巨型狗鱼、梭鲈和大拟鲈，夏季租一栋湖畔红木屋（Mökki）出钓是芬兰式生活的巅峰。
          - **托尔讷河（River Tornionjoki - 拉普兰）**：欧洲最长、完全未修筑大坝的野生大西洋鲑洄游大河。夏至前后坐在传统木船上顺流顺水放拖钓（Harling），动辄捕获 15–20 公斤的巨型野鲑。
          - **伊纳里湖（Lake Inari）**：北极圈深处的旷野圣湖，水质纯净达直接饮用级别。出产北极红点鲑、湖鳟与背鳍如帆的北极茴鱼。
