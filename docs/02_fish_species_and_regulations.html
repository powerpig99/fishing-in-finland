🐟 Finland's Top Target Species: Biology & Catch Limits

Understanding statutory minimum catch sizes (alamitta) and closed spawning seasons (rauhoitusaika) is an absolute prerequisite for legal and ethical fishing in Finland. Taking an undersized or protected fish carries strict statutory fines determined per individual specimen by the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry.

🔍 How to Check the Adipose Fin (Rasvaevä) on Trout

When you land any trout in Finnish waters, your very first action before measuring length is to inspect the small fleshy fin located on the dorsal ridge between the main dorsal fin and the tail:

  • Adipose Fin Present (Intact): This is a wild trout (luonnontaimen). In all Finnish maritime waters and anywhere south of 64°N latitude, wild trout are strictly protected year-round. Keep the fish in the water, gently remove the hook with long forceps, and release it immediately.
  • Adipose Fin Missing (Neat scar): This is a hatchery-stocked fish (istukas) clipped as a juvenile. If it meets the statutory minimum length of 50 cm, you may legally keep it.
Statutory Value of Protected Fish: Illegally killing an adipose-intact wild sea trout carries a mandatory compensatory conservation fine of €3,260 per fish under Finnish criminal law, plus revocation of fishing privileges.

🔍 关键实操：如何鉴别鳟鱼的“脂鳍（Rasvaevä）”

在芬兰水域（特别是波罗的海与南部水系）钓获任何鳟鱼时，起竿第一件事绝不是量长度，而是立刻查看脂鳍（位于背鳍后方、尾鳍前方背脊上的肉质小鳍）：

  • 脂鳍完整存在：属于野生海鳟/野生褐鳟。在芬兰全部海域及北纬64度以南内陆水域，全年全境绝对禁捕！必须在水下直接用摘钩钳卸钩并放归，不得离水摔在碎石上。
  • 脂鳍位置缺失（平整切口愈合痕迹）：属于人工增殖放流鱼。若体长测量达到或超过 50 cm，方可合法保留。
违法代价极高： 依据芬兰法律，非法私藏或杀害一条野生完整脂鳍海鳟，除面临刑事扣押捕钓装备外，必须依法补缴每条 3,260 欧元的法定生态惩罚补偿金。
