## English Version

## 🧰 Visual Equipment Guide: Gear & Tackle Decoded

  
To ensure you know exactly what gear to look for in Finnish tackle stores (like Motonet, Ruoto, or Prisma), browse the visual equipment gallery below with exact Finnish names and functions:

  

    
    
### Teleskooppionkivapa &amp; Onkilaite
![Telescopic Pole &amp; Onkilaite Rig](../../images/gear_onki_kit.svg)
*4m–5m Pole + Pre-tied Rig + Live Earthworms*

The ultimate zero-barrier entry point in Finland. No reel, no casting tangles, no license required. Telescopic pole collapses to 1.1m for bus transit. Catch perch, roach, and bream from day one.

    
  
    
    
### Avokelasetti (Spinning Combo)
![Spinning Rod and Reel Combo](../../images/gear_rod_reel.jpg)
*7ft (210cm) Rod + 2500 Spinning Reel*

The universal Finnish starter setup. Medium-Light rod (cast weight 7–25g) with a front-drag 2500 reel. Spooled with 0.12–0.14mm 8-strand braid and a 0.35mm fluorocarbon leader.

    

    
    
### Jigipäät ja Jigit (Jigs &amp; Softbaits)
![Jighead and Softbaits](../../images/gear_jigs.svg)
*Round Lead/Tungsten Heads (7g–15g) + 2.5"–3.5" Shads*

Silicone paddle tail shads mounted onto single hooks with round weights. The tail flutters energetically on the fall, triggering reflexive predator strikes.

    

    
    
### Lusikkauistin (Casting Spoon)
![Kuusamo Metal Spoon](../../images/gear_spoon.jpg)
*Kuusamo Professor / Räsänen Style (15g–25g)*

Curved brass spoon stamped in Finland. Flutters with an erratic wobbling action on slow retrieve and sinks with a backward glide when paused. Copper/silver is the top color.

    

    
    
### Vaappu (Balsa / Plastic Wobbler)
![Rapala Balsa Wobbler](../../images/gear_wobbler.jpg)
*Rapala Original / Countdown (7cm–11cm)*

Floating or sinking minnow lure with a plastic diving lip. Produces the legendary 'wounded minnow' wobble invented by Lauri Rapala on Lake Päijänne.

    

    
    
### Silakanlitka (Herring Sabiki Rig)
![Silakanlitka Herring Rig](../../images/gear_silakanlitka.svg)
*6–8 Shiny Gold Hooks + 35g Sinker*

Unbaited multi-hook string jigged vertically from bridges in May–June. Catches full strings of Baltic herring without any live bait.

    

    
    
### Jääkaira (Hand Ice Auger)
![Hand Ice Auger](../../images/gear_ice_auger.jpg)
*130mm (5") or 150mm (6") Spiral Hand Auger*

Razor-sharp curved steel blades that drill through 30 cm of hard lake ice in 20 seconds. 130mm is the universal size for perch; 150mm for larger pike and zander.

    

    
    
### Jäänaskalit (Ice Safety Picks)
![Ice Safety Picks](../../images/gear_ice_picks.svg)
*Twin Hardened Steel Spikes with Neck Cord*

Always worn around the neck outside your winter coat. If you fall through thin ice, you grip a pick in each hand and stab the ice to pull yourself out safely.

    

    
    
### Pilkkivapa &amp; Tasuri (Ice Rod &amp; Balance Jig)
![Ice Rod and Balance Jig](../../images/gear_ice_rod.jpg)
*Compact Winter Rod + Horizontal Finnish Lure*

Short, sensitive ice rod. When jigged, the horizontal balance jig (*tasapainopilkki*) darts in wide figure-8 circles beneath the ice hole to entice large winter perch.

    

    
    
### Havashaavi, Pappi &amp; Filetti
![Landing Net and Priest](../../images/gear_landing_net.svg)
*Rubber Mesh Net, Stunning Priest &amp; Filleting Knife*

Rubber netting protects fish mucus membrane during catch and release. The wooden priest delivers an instant humane blow (*pappaus*) for dinner fish.

    
  

  
## 🪢 Essential Knots: The Double Uni Knot Tutorial

  
You only need to master two knots to fish successfully in Finland: connecting zero-stretch braided mainline to fluorocarbon leader (Double Uni Knot), and tying your terminal snap swivel (Palomar Knot).

### 📋 The Exact €10–€15 Onkiminen Shopping Checklist

  
Take this checklist with Finnish terminology to the store or search on motonet.fi / prisma.fi:

  
    - **Telescopic Pole:** Ask for *Teleskooppionkivapa, 5 metriä* (~€8.00–€10.00). A 5m pole is the golden length for shore fishing, reaching past pier pylons and rock edges.
    - **Ready-Made Float Rig:** Look for *Valmis onkilaite* (~€2.50–€3.00). Comes wound on an H-shaped plastic ladder winder with 5m line, float, calibrated lead split shots, and a pre-tied #10–#12 hook.
    - **Live Earthworms:** Look inside the bait fridge for *Kastematoja (purkki)* (~€3.90 for ~12 large Canadian nightcrawlers) or *Onkimatoja* (~€3.50 for ~25 medium garden worms). Keep cool out of direct sunlight.
    - **Disgorger / Pliers:** *Koukunirrotin* (~€2.00) or small needle-nose pliers for unhooking fish quickly and gently.
    - **Plastic Bucket:** *Sanko / Ämpäri* (~€1.50) to hold clean lake water for fish or bait.

## 🧰 必备装备实物高清图鉴与选购对照

  
为了让您在芬兰当地渔具店（Motonet、Ruoto、Prisma）选购时一目了然，以下为核心装备的实物高清图鉴、芬兰语专属名称与实战功能：

  

    
    
### Teleskooppionkivapa &amp; Onkilaite（伸缩手竿与成品线组）
![传统手竿与成品线组](../../images/gear_onki_kit.svg)
*4–5米伸缩竿 + 成品浮漂主线组 + 活蚯蚓盒*

芬兰最省心省钱的入门首选。无绕线轮、零炸线打结风险、无需任何钓鱼证件。缩回后仅1.1米便于携带搭乘公交地铁。第一天出钓即可稳定起鱼。

    
  
    
    
### Avokelasetti（路亚纺车竿轮套装）
![路亚直柄竿轮套装](../../images/gear_rod_reel.jpg)
*7尺（210cm）直柄竿 + 2500 型前泄力纺车轮*

芬兰最通用的入门首选。中轻调（ML）竿（饵重 7–25g），搭配 0.12–0.14mm 8编PE线与 0.35mm 碳线前导，可轻松应对全芬90%的水域。

    

    
    
### Jigipäät ja Jigit（铅头钩与硅胶软鱼）
![软虫与铅头钩组](../../images/gear_jigs.svg)
*7g–15g 钨钢/铅头钩 + 2.5寸–3.5寸 T尾/卷尾软虫*

软虫穿在带倒刺铅头钩上。抛投触底后一抬一顿，软虫在紧线下落时鱼尾剧烈高频摆动，诱发凶猛咬口。

    

    
    
### Lusikkauistin（金属勺形亮片）
![芬兰库萨莫勺形亮片](../../images/gear_spoon.jpg)
*Kuusamo Professor / Räsänen 经典款（15g–25g）*

芬兰百年冲压黄铜勺形亮片。慢速收线时呈现大幅度不规则摇摆，一收一顿间后翻下沉。双面铜银色（kupari/hopea）是全芬公认杀手色。

    

    
    
### Vaappu（巴尔沙木/塑料米诺假饵）
![Rapala 巴尔沙木米诺假饵](../../images/gear_wobbler.jpg)
*Rapala Original Floater / Countdown（7cm–11cm）*

带有潜水压水板的仿生木质硬饵。完美再现创始人劳里·乐伯乐（Lauri Rapala）在芬兰湖泊发明的“受伤小鱼垂死挣扎”传奇泳姿。

    

    
    
### Silakanlitka（波罗的海鲱鱼串钩组）
![鲱鱼串钩](../../images/gear_silakanlitka.svg)
*6–8 枚金色反光小钩 + 35g 水滴铅坠*

完全不挂饵的排钩钓组。每年5-6月站在跨海大桥上垂直上下提拉，利用钩身反光诱骗成群洄游鲱鱼，一竿拉起串串爆桶。

    

    
    
### Jääkaira（手摇冰钻）
![手摇冰钻](../../images/gear_ice_auger.jpg)
*130mm（5寸）或 150mm（6寸）锋利螺旋手钻*

配备极锋利弧形合金刀片，20秒即可平稳凿穿30厘米厚实坚冰。130mm是五道黑黄金口径，150mm适合狗鱼与大梭鲈。

    

    
    
### Jäänaskalit（冰上安全救生防溺冰锥）
![冰上救生防溺手锥](../../images/gear_ice_picks.svg)
*双合金尖刺塑料手柄 + 胸前防丢挂绳*

必须始终挂在冬装外套胸前！万一踏破薄冰落水，双手紧握冰锥猛扎冰面即可借力爬回坚固冰层，是绝对的生命底线。

    

    
    
### Pilkkivapa ja Tasuri（冰钓短竿与平衡小鱼）
![冰钓短竿与芬兰平衡小鱼](../../images/gear_ice_rod.jpg)
*便携式冬钓短竿 + 芬兰本土原产平衡拟饵*

轻灵手持短竿搭配水平平衡小鱼（Tasuri）。轻轻一提，假饵在冰下沿8字形大范围回旋游弋，专诱大体型五道黑。

    

    
    
### Havashaavi, Pappi ja Filetti
![抄网与击鱼棒剔骨刀](../../images/gear_landing_net.svg)
*硅胶无结抄网、木质击鱼棒与柔性剔骨刀*

无结硅胶网保护放流鱼粘膜；击鱼棒（Kalapappi）瞬间击晕处死目标鱼，剔骨刀快速割鳃放血并剔出无刺白肉鱼排。
