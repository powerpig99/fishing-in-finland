# 03. Modern Fishing Techniques & Seasonal Tactics
*03. 现代作钓技法全解析与季节性战术 • 03. Kalastusmuodot, modernit tekniikat ja välineet*

> **Reading Time**: 9 min  
> **Sub-topics**: Jigging for perch & zander, spoons and spinners for pike, spring bottom angling for whitefish, herring sabiki rigs, and winter ice fishing.

## English Version

## 🎯 1. Jigging & Soft Plastics (Jigaus) — The Modern Perch & Zander Weapon

        
Jigging with silicone soft baits (shads, grub tails, pin tails) mounted on tungsten or lead jigheads is by far the most popular and productive summer and autumn method in Finland. It accounts for the majority of perch and zander catches.

        
          - **Traditional Bottom Jigging (*Pohjajigaus*)**: Cast out, let the jig touch bottom (watch your braided line go slack), make 2–3 quick turns of the reel handle to bounce the bait off the bottom, then pause on a tight line. 90% of strikes occur during this fluttering drop.
          - **Dropshot Rig**: Exceptional in autumn when perch gather around deep drop-offs and bridge pilings in Helsinki and Tampere. Keeps the lure suspended 30–50 cm above silty bottoms without snagging.
          - **Optimal Gear**: A fast-action 6'6"–7' rod (cast weight 5–20g), thin 8-carrier braided line (0.10–0.12mm), and a 40–50 cm fluorocarbon shock leader (0.28–0.35mm).
        

        
## 🥄 2. Classic Finnish Spoons & Spinners (Heittokalastus)

        
Finland has a century-long tradition of world-renowned metal lure craftsmanship tailored for northern pike, perch, and trout:

        
          - **Kuusamo Professor & Räsänen**: The gold standard for pike. These wide, stamped brass spoons flutter erratically on a slow retrieve and flutter backwards when paused. Essential colors: copper/silver (*kupari/hopea*), perch pattern, and firetiger.
          - **Blue Fox Vibrax & Mepps Aglia**: In-line spinners with a spinning blade creating heavy sonic vibrations. Sizes #2 and #3 in copper and black-with-yellow-dots are legendary for Finnish perch; sizes #4 and #5 entice shallow pike and river trout.
          - **Rapala Wobblers (*Vaaput*)**: Original Floater and Countdown in 7–11 cm lengths imitate injured vendace (*muikku*) or bleak along rocky shorelines at dusk.
        

        
## 🏖️ 3. Spring Bottom Angling for Whitefish (Siianonginta)

        
Immediately after the coastal sea ice breaks up in April and May, hundreds of anglers head to the rocky headlands and sandy beaches of Helsinki, Espoo, and Porkkalanniemi for *siianonginta*:

        
          - **The Rig**: A 9–10ft medium spinning rod, running sinker (20–40g bell sinker on a sliding clip), bead, swivel, and a 60–80 cm fine fluorocarbon leader tied to a small #6 or #8 baitholder hook.
          - **The Bait**: Fresh garden earthworms (*kastemato*). Whitefish cruise shallow sandy bottoms searching for marine worms and crustaceans awakened by the warming spring sun.
          - **The Setup**: Cast 25–40 meters out onto sandy bottoms, place the rod in a bank stick with line taut, and hang a small bell or watch the rod tip bounce sharply.
        

        
## 🌉 4. Baltic Herring Sabiki Jigging (Silakanlitkaus)

        
From mid-May to early June (and again in October), immense shoals of Baltic herring enter the inner archipelago to spawn in coastal channels. The Lauttasaari bridge (*Lauttasaarensilta*) in Helsinki becomes a bustling social hub:

        
          - **Rig**: A specialized herring rig (*silakanlitka*, costs €2–€3 at any supermarket or Motonet). It features a 30–50g pear sinker at the bottom and 6–10 small, shiny, barbless golden hooks tied on short droppers.
          - **Technique**: Drop the rig vertically straight down from the bridge or pier until it hits bottom. Lift the rod up smoothly 1 meter, let it sink back down, and repeat. You will quickly feel the energetic vibrations of 4–8 herring hooked simultaneously!
          - **Legal Note**: Completely free under General Fishing Rights. You can catch 10–20 kg in a single afternoon for family smoking or pickling.
        

        
## ❄️ 5. Winter Ice Fishing (Pilkintä)

        
When Finnish waters freeze solid between December and April, ice fishing becomes the nation's premier winter outdoor ritual:

        
          - **Vertical Spoons & Mormyshkas (*Pystypilkit ja Morrit*)**: Drop a small teardrop tungsten mormyshka tipped with 2–3 red maggots (*kärpäsentoukka*) down to the bottom and twitch gently. Perfect for winter perch and roach.
          - **Balance Jigs (*Tasapainopilkit*)**: Horizontal minnow-style lures with hooks on nose and tail that swim in wide expanding circles when jerked. Irresistible to larger predator perch and pike.
          - **Safety Essentials**: Always wear ice safety picks (*jäänaskalit*) around your neck! Never venture onto ice thinner than 5–7 cm of solid clear blue ice.

## 中文版 (Chinese Version)

## 🎯 1. 软饵跳底路亚（Jigaus）—— 征服五道黑与梭鲈的现代利器

        
在芬兰，使用硅胶软虫（T尾、卷尾、针尾）搭配钨钢/铅头钩的跳底钓法（*Jigaus*）是夏季与秋季最主流、渔获量最高的技法，占到了鲈鱼和梭鲈总捕获量的大半以上。

        
          - **经典触底跳底（*Pohjajigaus*）**：抛投后静候铅头沉底（紧盯PE编织线突然松弛塌下即为触底），随后快速转动线轮2–3圈使软虫脱离水底向上窜起跳跃，然后暂停收线保持紧线状态让软虫沿弧线滑行下坠。90%以上的凶猛咬口都发生在软虫紧线悬垂下落的黄金两秒钟。
          - **倒吊钓组（Dropshot）**：深秋水温骤降、大鲈鱼退守深坑或桥墩立柱时的必杀技。将铅坠沉于底部，软虫横向系于其上方 30–50 cm 处，可在原地轻微抖动而不挂底泥，长时间诱惑活性极低的鱼群。
          - **黄金推荐参数**：快调（Fast Action）6.6至7尺路亚竿（饵重 5–20g），0.10–0.12mm 优质8编PE线，前导必须接 40–50 cm 的 0.28–0.35mm 碳素线（耐磨抗刮并隐形）。
        

        
## 🥄 2. 芬兰百年经典铁板亮片与旋转亮片（Heittokalastus）

        
芬兰是全世界金属亮片制造工艺的翘楚，针对北国水域的狗鱼与红点鲑有着近百年的独家设计基因：

        
          - **Kuusamo Professor（库萨莫教授）与 Räsänen**：狗鱼界的传世宗师。大弧度冲压黄铜勺形亮片，在极慢速收线时呈现大幅度不规则摇摆，一收一顿间能产生独特的反向后翻下潜。最杀手涂装：银铜双色（*kupari/hopea*）、五道黑条纹色（perch）以及火虎色（firetiger）。
          - **Blue Fox Vibrax（蓝狐旋转亮片）与 Mepps Aglia**：带有声波铜铃内腔的旋转亮片。2号与3号金铜色或黑底黄点涂装是全芬兰抓取大五道黑的传奇；4号与5号大尺寸则专攻近岸浅滩的狗鱼与溪流红点鲑。
          - **Rapala 经典悬浮米诺（*Vaaput*）**：Original Floater 与 Countdown 7–11 cm 系列，在黄昏暮色下沿石礁浅滩慢拖，完美模拟芬兰白鲑幼鱼（*muikku*）垂死挣扎的姿态。
        

        
## 🏖️ 3. 早春沿海沙滩沉底钓白鲑（Siianonginta）

        
每年4月至5月波罗的海海冰刚刚消融之际，整个大赫尔辛基地区（埃斯波、赫尔辛基沿岸、Porkkalanniemi）最标志性的户外盛事便是“沙滩沉底钓白鲑（*siianonginta*）”：

        
          - **钓组配置**：9–10尺中硬度海竿或抛竿，主线上套 20–40g 水滴通心活坠，加防撞胶豆与八字环，下接 60–80 cm 细碳线前导，绑 6号或8号带倒刺蚯蚓钩。
          - **绝杀诱饵**：新鲜红蚯蚓（*kastemato*）。春季阳光晒暖浅海沙滩，白鲑成群游弋于水深1–3米沙泥底质觅食沙蚕与微型甲壳类。
          - **作钓手法**：将钓组抛向距岸25–40米的浅海沙泥平滩，竿插架于岸边沙地，收紧虚线，竿尖夹上小铜铃或静候竿尖大幅连续下弯。
        

        
## 🌉 4. 桥头波罗的海鲱鱼串钩（Silakanlitkaus）

        
每年5月中旬至6月上旬（以及深秋10-11月），巨量波罗的海小鲱鱼洄游穿越赫尔辛基群岛狭窄水道。赫尔辛基连接市区与Lauttasaari大岛的跨海大桥（*Lauttasaarensilta*）上瞬间站满钓友：

        
          - **串钩钓组（Silakanlitka）**：在各大超市或 Motonet 仅需2-3欧元即可买到的成品串钩。底部配 30–50g 水滴铅坠，主线上密布 6–10 枚极微小的反光金银无倒刺裸钩，完全不需要挂任何饵料！
          - **操作技巧**：站在桥栏边将铅坠垂直沉入水底，抬起竿尖向上提拉1米，随后松线自由落体，反复有节奏提拉。数秒内便能感到整根鱼竿剧烈震颤，一竿拉起即可串起4至8条银光闪烁的肥嫩鲱鱼！
          - **法律提醒**：完全属于自然民权免费项目！半天即可轻松钓获10–20公斤，全家油炸、烟熏或醋渍极为鲜美。
        

        
## ❄️ 5. 芬兰冬日传统冰钓（Pilkintä）

        
12月至次年4月全芬兰千里冰封之时，冰钓是芬兰冬季最重要的国民生活仪式：

        
          - **垂直冰钓亮片与微铅头（Morri）**：使用短手把冰钓竿，下放系有2-3条红虫/面包虫的钨钢米粒钩（*morri*），在距底5-10cm处微微颤动，是严冬五道黑与拟鲤的绝杀。
          - **平衡拟饵（Tasapainopilkki）**：带有前后双钩与腹部三叉钩的横向小鱼拟饵，轻轻一提便能在冰下沿8字形大范围回旋游动，专诱大体型掠食性鲈鱼和潜伏狗鱼。
          - **安全铁律**：脖子上必须随时挂着**冰爪救生锥（*jäänaskalit*）**！未达到 5–7 厘米厚坚固纯蓝冰严禁冒险踏冰。

## Suomeksi (Finnish Version)

## 🎯 1. Jigaus — Moderni ahvenen ja kuhan pyynti

        
Pehmeillä jigeillä ja jigipäillä kalastaminen on noussut Suomessa suosituimmaksi avovesikalastusmuodoksi. Saaliiksi saadaan erityisesti ahventa ja kuhaa.

        
          - **Pohjajigaus**: Heitä jigi, anna sen vajota pohjaan, kelaa 2–3 kammenkierrosta ja pidä siima tiukalla vajoamisen ajan. Tärppi tulee lähes poikkeuksetta vajoamisvaiheessa.
          - **Dropshot**: Erinomainen kylmän veden aikaan syyskuukausina syvänteissä ja siltapilareilla, jolloin jigiä voidaan värisyttää pohjan yläpuolella ilman tarttumista liejuun.
        

        
## 🥄 2. Klassiset suomalaiset lusikat ja lipat

        
          - **Kuusamo Professor ja Räsänen**: Haukikalastuksen kruunaamattomat kuninkaat laajalla potkulla ja hitaalla uppoamisella.
          - **Blue Fox Vibrax ja Mepps**: Lipat, joiden kuparinen tai mustapohjainen lehti houkuttelee ahventa tehokkaasti.
          - **Rapala-vaaput**: Original Floating ja Countdown matalissa kivikkorannoissa auringonlaskun aikaan.
        

        
## 🏖️ 3. Kevään siian pohjaonginta

        
Heti jäiden lähdettyä huhti-toukokuussa rannikon hiekkarannoille suunnataan siian pohjaongintaan. Liukupaino (20–40g), tapsi ja matosyötti hiekkapohjalle heitettynä tuottaa erinomaista ruokasiikaa.

        
## 🌉 4. Silakanlitkaus

        
Toukokuussa ja lokakuussa salmiin saapuvat valtavat silakkaparvet. Lauttasaaren sillalta litkaaminen (paino ja 6–10 kirkasta koukkua) on perinteikäs ja maksuton yleiskalastusoikeus.

        
## ❄️ 5. Talvinen pilkintä

        
Pystypilkit, tasapainopilkit ja morrit tarjoavat tehokkaan tavan tavoittaa ahvenia ja haukia talvella. Jäänaskalit ovat aina pakollinen hengenpelastusvaruste kaulalla.
