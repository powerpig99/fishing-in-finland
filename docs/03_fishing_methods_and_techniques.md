## English Version

## 🎯 1. Jigging & Soft Plastics (Jigaus) — Trajectory & Mechanics

  
Jigging with silicone soft baits mounted on tungsten or lead jigheads is by far the most productive summer and autumn method for perch and zander in Finland. To master it, understand how the lure moves along the seabed:

  
![Bottom Jigging Trajectory Diagram](../../images/jigging_technique_diagram.svg)
*Figure 3.1: The Bottom Jigging Cycle (Pohjajigaus): 1. Feel bottom touch, 2. Two sharp reel turns hop lure 0.5–1m, 3. Tight-line gliding fall where 90% of strikes occur.*

  
> 🎬 **[🎬 Verified Video: Ahvenen Jigikalastus – Vinkit, Välineet ja Tekniikat (Eumer Fishing)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=REsf5QnOHE0)**
> Comprehensive breakdown of jighead weights, soft plastic colors, rod twitch cadence, watching the braided line, and feeling strikes on the fall.
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=REsf5QnOHE0)

  

  
## 🏖️ 2. Spring Bottom Angling for Whitefish (Siianonginta)

  
Immediately after coastal sea ice melts in April and May, anglers head to sandy beaches in Helsinki, Espoo, and Porkkalanniemi. Study the exact terminal rig and action below:

  
![Spring Whitefish Rig Diagram](../../images/siianonginta_rig_diagram.svg)
*Figure 3.2: Terminal Siianonginta Rig: Running pear sinker (20–40g), rubber buffer bead, swivel, 60–80 cm fluorocarbon leader, and baitholder hook with live earthworm.*

> Real-world beach setup: running sinker knotting, baiting fresh earthworms, rod placement, and recognizing delicate spring whitefish taps.

## 🌉 3. Baltic Herring Sabiki Jigging (Silakanlitkaus)

  
In May–June and October, immense shoals of Baltic herring enter the inner archipelago channels. Using a specialized 6-to-8 hook bare gold rig (*silakanlitka*) with a 35g sinker, you jig vertically from bridges.

  
> 🎬 **[🎬 Verified Video: Silakanlitkaus Helsingissä (Helsinki Street Fishing)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XmPNPsNn2K0)**
> The iconic Helsinki bridge spectacle: dropping the unbaited litka rig straight down into tidal currents, rhythmic vertical lifting, and bringing up full strings of shimmering herring. Free under general rights!
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XmPNPsNn2K0)

  

  
## ❄️ 4. Winter Ice Fishing (Pilkintä) & Safety Protocol

  
From December to April, Finnish waters freeze solid. Learn how to drill clean holes, work balance jigs and mormyshkas, and wear safety picks.

  
> 🎬 **[🎬 Verified Video: Ahvenen Pilkintä ja Tekniikat (Eumer Fishing)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jqkjIp3gPmM)**
> Hand auger drilling, finding lake drop-offs, working tungsten mormyshkas with live maggots, and working balance jigs for predator perch.
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jqkjIp3gPmM)

## 🎯 1. 软虫跳底路亚（Jigaus）—— 触底轨迹与动作精要

  
在芬兰，软虫跳底是斩获五道黑（鲈鱼）与梭鲈效率最高的手法。通过以下清晰轨迹图，洞悉拟饵在水底的真实游姿：

  
![软虫跳底触底轨迹示意图](../../images/jigging_technique_diagram.svg)
*图 3.1：软虫跳底循环（Pohjajigaus）：1. 抛投沉底线松弛；2. 快速收线两圈将软饵弹起0.5-1米；3. 绷紧主线滑翔下落，90%的攻击均发生在下落瞬间。*

  
> 🎬 **[🎬 官方正版视频教学：芬兰五道黑与梭鲈软虫跳底实战精讲（Eumer Fishing 官方出品）](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=REsf5QnOHE0)**
> 详解软虫选型、铅头克重匹配、紧盯PE编织线突然塌软看触底，以及感知软虫下落滑翔中那一声清脆的“顿口”。
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=REsf5QnOHE0)

  

  
## 🏖️ 2. 早春沙滩沉底钓白鲑（Siianonginta）精细线组

  
每年4-5月海冰刚消融之际，整个大赫尔辛基地区最标志性的户外盛事便是“沙滩沉底钓白鲑”。请严格参考下方经典钓组：

  
![春季沙滩沉底白鲑钓组图解](../../images/siianonginta_rig_diagram.svg)
*图 3.2：沙滩沉底白鲑线组：通心水滴铅坠（20–40g）、防撞缓冲胶豆、八字环、60–80cm 隐形碳线前导与带倒刺蚯蚓钩。*

  
> 🎬 **[🎬 官方正版视频教学：春季通心铅沙滩沉底白鲑实景绑组与作钓（Eumer Fishing）](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e61LcTPdwas)**
> 完整展示沙洲点位选择、远投30米入沙底、架竿紧线、观察竿尖微弱点动咬口，以及起获白鲑全过程。
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e61LcTPdwas)

  

  
## 🌉 3. 跨海大桥波罗的海鲱鱼串钩（Silakanlitkaus）

  
每年5-6月和深秋10月，赫尔辛基 Lauttasaari 跨海大桥上站满钓友。使用2-3欧元的专用串钩，完全无需挂饵，垂直上下提拉即可爆桶。

  
> 🎬 **[🎬 现场实录视频：赫尔辛基沿海水域鲱鱼串钩提拉实景（Helsinki Street Fishing）](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XmPNPsNn2K0)**
> 沉底、提竿、自由下落，一竿串起多尾银鳞小鲱鱼！全自然民权免费，新手半天即可满载而归。
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XmPNPsNn2K0)

  

  
## ❄️ 4. 芬兰冬日传统冰钓（Pilkintä）与安全生命线

  
12月至次年4月千里冰封，学习如何安全辨识冰层厚度、钻孔，以及使用钨钢米粒钩与平衡小鱼诱钓深水大鲈。

  
> 🎬 **[🎬 官方正版视频教学：芬兰冬季冰钓大师课与引诱技法（Eumer Fishing）](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jqkjIp3gPmM)**
> 手摇冰钻使用诀窍、红虫挂钩法，以及平衡拟饵在冰下的8字回旋诱鱼技法。
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jqkjIp3gPmM)
