03. Modern Fishing Techniques, Rig Diagrams & Video Walkthroughs

Jigging trajectories, soft plastics, spring bottom whitefish rig diagram, herring sabiki rigs, and curated video masterclasses.

🎯 1. Jigging & Soft Plastics (Jigaus) — Trajectory & Mechanics

Jigging with silicone soft baits mounted on tungsten or lead jigheads is by far the most productive summer and autumn method for perch and zander in Finland. To master it, understand how the lure moves along the seabed:

🎬 Verified Video: Ahvenen Jigikalastus – Vinkit, Välineet ja Tekniikat (Eumer Fishing)
Comprehensive breakdown of jighead weights, soft plastic colors, rod twitch cadence, watching the braided line, and feeling strikes on the fall.

🏖️ 2. Spring Bottom Angling for Whitefish (Siianonginta)

Immediately after coastal sea ice melts in April and May, anglers head to sandy beaches in Helsinki, Espoo, and Porkkalanniemi. Study the exact terminal rig and action below:

🎬 Verified Video: Siikaa Pohjaongella – Siian Onginta, Liukupaino ja Tapsi (Eumer Fishing)
Real-world beach setup: running sinker knotting, baiting fresh earthworms, rod placement, and recognizing delicate spring whitefish taps.

🌉 3. Baltic Herring Sabiki Jigging (Silakanlitkaus)

In May–June and October, immense shoals of Baltic herring enter the inner archipelago channels. Using a specialized 6-to-8 hook bare gold rig (silakanlitka) with a 35g sinker, you jig vertically from bridges.

🎬 Verified Video: Silakanlitkaus Helsingissä (Helsinki Street Fishing)
The iconic Helsinki bridge spectacle: dropping the unbaited litka rig straight down into tidal currents, rhythmic vertical lifting, and bringing up full strings of shimmering herring. Free under general rights!

❄️ 4. Winter Ice Fishing (Pilkintä) & Safety Protocol

From December to April, Finnish waters freeze solid. Learn how to drill clean holes, work balance jigs and mormyshkas, and wear safety picks.

🎬 Verified Video: Ahvenen Pilkintä ja Tekniikat (Eumer Fishing)
Hand auger drilling, finding lake drop-offs, working tungsten mormyshkas with live maggots, and working balance jigs for predator perch.
