## English Version

## 📅 12 Months on Finnish Waters: The Seasonal Rhythms

        
In the high latitudes of the Nordic subarctic, fish activity is dictated by extreme swings in daylight, water temperature, and ice dynamics. Aligning your efforts with the seasonal calendar turns unpredictable outings into consistently rewarding adventures.

        
          
| Month | Water & Ice Conditions | Primary Target Species | Top Techniques | Key Focus & Tactics |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **January** | Thick solid ice (20–40 cm) | Burbot (*Made*), Perch | Night bottom ice-jigging; Tungsten mormyshkas | Peak spawning time for burbot. Head onto ice after 6 PM with a headlamp; bottom jig near gravel bars. |
| **February** | Deep winter, snow-covered ice | Perch, Pike, Burbot | Balance jigs (*tasuri*), Tip-ups (*ootit*) | Perch school tightly in deep lake depressions (6–12m). Target drop-offs at mid-day when light is brightest. |
| **March** | Long sunny spring days; thick ice | Perch, Whitefish, Arctic Char | Vertical spoons & mormyshkas | "Kevätpilkintä" (spring ice fishing). Sun warms shallow bays under clear ice; perch feed voraciously. |
| **April** | Ice melts along coast; rivers open | Whitefish (*Siika*), Sea Trout | Spring bottom angling (*Siianonginta*); Shore spoons | Coastal ice-out! Cast worms on sandy beaches (Helsinki/Espoo) for whitefish; cast spoons for hungry sea trout. |
| **May** | Warming shallow bays (8–14°C) | Baltic Herring, Pike, Perch | Herring sabiki rigs (*litka*); Shallow weedless spoons | Lauttasaari bridge packed with herring anglers. Post-spawn pike feed aggressively in warm reed shallows. |
| **June** | Midnight Sun, summer begins | Zander (*Kuha*), Perch, Salmon | Evening jigging; Trolling; River rapids | Zander season explodes! Fish calm warm bays during the midnight twilight (22:00–02:00). Torne River salmon run begins. |
| **July** | Warmest water (18–24°C), holiday season | Perch, Zander, Pike | Deep jigging; Surface poppers; Cottage angling | Fish morning and late dusk. Midday heat drives zander and perch deeper into submerged weed lines and trenches. |
| **August** | Warm water cools slightly; darkening nights | Perch, Zander, Crayfish (*Rapu*) | Shad jigging; Dropshot; Crayfish traps | Crayfish season opens (starts 21 July at noon). Perch gather in massive schools feeding on young-of-the-year fry. |
| **September** | Autumn chill, water cools to 12°C | Trophy Perch, Pike, Sea Trout | Bottom jigging (10–15g); Heavy spoons | Prime time for giant perch over 1 kg. River trout enter closed season (1 Sept). Sea trout cruise coastal rocks. |
| **October** | Crisp autumn, stormy sea | Northern Pike (Monsters!), Baltic Herring | Big rubber shads (18–25 cm); Herring litka | The month of monster pike! Big females gorge before winter. Autumn herring run brings second bounty to bridges. |
| **November** | Freezing air, shoreline ice forms | Sea Trout, Burbot | Coastal surf casting; Night bottom fishing | Hardcore season. Cast long slim spoons into freezing Baltic surf for silvery sea trout. Waters begin to skim over. |
| **December** | First ice (*Ensijäät*) forms | Perch, Burbot | Early winter ice fishing | **EXTREME CAUTION ON FIRST ICE!** Never walk on ice under 5 cm. Early ice offers explosive perch bites. |

## 中文版 (Chinese Version)

## 📅 芬兰水域十二月节律：物候与鱼类生理周期

        
在北欧亚寒带高纬度地区，鱼类的觅食、集群与生殖行为完全受制于日照时长、水温剧变与冰冻周期的掌控。掌握这套四季生物节律，是每一位在芬兰长期生活的钓手从“碰运气”升华至“游刃有余”的必由之路。

        
          
| 月份 | 水温与冰层物候 | 当月核心主攻鱼种 | 最具杀伤力技法 | 出钓战术与注意事项 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **一月（Tammikuu）** | 千里冰封，冰层厚达 20–40 厘米 | 江鳕（山鲶鱼）、五道黑 | 黑夜沉底重铅跳底；微型米粒铅头（Morri） | 淡水鳕鱼（江鳕）产卵大爆发。傍晚6点戴头灯上冰，在砂砾乱石浅坑底钓，鱼肉嫩美、鱼肝极肥。 |
| **二月（Helmikuu）** | 深冬隆冬，冰面积雪极厚，水温逼近0℃ | 五道黑、狗鱼、江鳕 | 平衡小鱼（Tasuri）、冰钓插竿（Ootit） | 五道黑紧缩在湖底6-12米深坑处越冬休眠。正午11点至下午2点光线最强时在深坑边缘下饵。 |
| **三月（Maaliskuu）** | 春阳高照，日照飞速延长，冰层最坚固 | 大五道黑、白鲑、北极红点鲑 | 垂直冰钓亮片搭挂红虫；平衡拟饵 | “春季冰钓（Kevätpilkintä）”黄金期！阳光穿透冰层晒暖浅水，五道黑苏醒并疯狂摄食。 |
| **四月（Huhtikuu）** | 沿海坚冰消融开江，南部活水河流解冻 | 白鲑（Siika）、海鳟 | **春季海滩沉底钓（Siianonginta）**；岸抛亮片 | 开江狂欢！在赫尔辛基/埃斯波向阳沙滩抛投活蚯蚓钓白鲑；在向海礁石区远投亮片搜寻海鳟。 |
| **五月（Toukokuu）** | 沿海浅湾升温（8–14℃），水草初生 | 波罗的海小鲱鱼、大白斑狗鱼 | 鲱鱼串钩（Litka）；浅滩防挂草狗鱼亮片 | Lauttasaari 跨海大桥鲱鱼狂欢月！产卵后极度饥饿的狗鱼聚集在近岸芦苇丛浅滩，极易爆竿。 |
| **六月（Kesäkuu）** | 极昼白夜，入夏水暖，仲夏节前后 | 梭鲈（Kuha）、大拟鲈、大西洋鲑 | 傍晚软虫跳底（Jigaus）；木船拖钓；急流飞蝇 | 梭鲈大军全线出动！晚上10点至凌晨2点微曦暮色中在避风海湾软虫跳底；北部托尔讷河鲑鱼大汛。 |
| **七月（Heinäkuu）** | 全年最高水温（18–24℃），全民木屋度假季 | 五道黑、梭鲈、狗鱼 | 深水暗礁跳底；水面波趴（Popper）；木屋休闲手竿 | 避开正午烈日，抓清晨与黄昏两头。正午大鱼多隐匿于深水水草根部或深坑暗礁处避暑。 |
| **八月（Elokuu）** | 黑夜回归，水温温和下行，捕小龙虾季 | 五道黑、梭鲈、小龙虾（Rapu） | 深浅结合软虫；倒吊（Dropshot）；地笼捕虾 | 7月21日正午法定小龙虾捕捞期开启。当年生小鱼成群，大体型五道黑结群追击，炸水频繁。 |
| **九月（Syyskuu）** | 秋意渐浓，气温骤降，水温跌至 12℃ | 公斤级巨鲈、大狗鱼、海鳟 | 10–15g 铅头重软虫触底；重型长条铁板 | 冲刺公斤级单尾巨型五道黑的最佳黄金月！注意：9月1日起内陆活水河流鳟鱼全面进入繁殖禁渔。 |
| **十月（Lokakuu）** | 深秋风暴频发，水体清冽刺骨 | 米级巨斑狗鱼、深秋波罗的海鲱鱼 | 18–25cm 特大硅胶软鱼；鲱鱼串钩 | 一年中钓获 80–110cm 巨型母狗鱼的最高概率月！大鲱鱼再次涌入群岛狭窄水道二次爆桶。 |
| **十一月（Marraskuu）** | 初霜初雪，岸边结薄冰，寒风呼啸 | 海鳟、江鳕 | 沿海怒涛远投亮片；夜间碎石底沉钓 | 真正的硬核钓友专属月。穿防水保温服在寒风大浪中大力抛投修长海鳟亮片，银白海鳟极其凶悍。 |
| **十二月（Joulukuu）** | 初冬初冰（Ensijäät）成型 | 五道黑、江鳕 | 初冰试探性冰钓 | **初冰安全第一铁律！** 纯蓝冰未达 5 厘米绝对严禁踏足！初冰下的五道黑活性惊人，常常满载而归。 |
