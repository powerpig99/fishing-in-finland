08. The Year-Round Finnish Fishing Calendar

Month-by-month guide: midwinter ice fishing, spring ice-out runs, whitefish beach angling, midnight sun zander, and autumn trophy pike.

📅 12 Months on Finnish Waters: Biological Rhythms

Fish activity in Finland is driven by extreme Nordic daylight and temperature swings. Here is your seasonal action calendar:

Month Water & Ice Conditions Primary Target Species Top Techniques Key Focus & Tactics
JanuarySolid ice (20–40 cm)Burbot (Made), PerchNight bottom jigging; MormyshkasBurbot peak spawning. Head onto ice after 6 PM with a headlamp near gravel bars.
FebruaryDeep snow on icePerch, Pike, BurbotBalance jigs (tasuri)Target deep depressions (6–12m) at midday when light penetrates ice.
MarchBright sunny days; thick icePerch, WhitefishVertical spoons & mormyshkas"Kevätpilkintä" spring ice fishing. Perch feed aggressively in warming shallows.
AprilIce breaks on coast; rivers openWhitefish (Siika), Sea TroutSpring bottom angling (Siianonginta)Open water begins! Cast worms on sandy beaches for whitefish; cast spoons for sea trout.
MayWarming bays (8–14°C)Baltic Herring, Pike, PerchHerring sabiki rigs (litka); SpoonsLauttasaari bridge packed with herring anglers. Pike feed aggressively in reed shallows.
JuneMidnight Sun, summer startsZander (Kuha), PerchEvening jigging; TrollingZander season explodes! Fish twilight midnight hours (22:00–02:00) in calm bays.
JulyWarm water (18–24°C), cottagesPerch, Zander, PikeDeep jigging; Surface poppersTarget morning and late dusk. Midday heat drives fish deeper to submerged trenches.
