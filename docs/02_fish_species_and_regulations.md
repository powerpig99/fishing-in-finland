## English Version

## 🐟 Finland's Top Target Species: Biology & Catch Limits

        
Understanding statutory minimum catch sizes (*alamitta*) and closed spawning seasons (*rauhoitusaika*) is an absolute prerequisite for legal and ethical fishing in Finland. Taking an undersized or protected fish carries strict statutory fines determined per individual specimen by the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry.

        
          
| Species (FI / EN / SCI) | Habitat in Finland | Minimum Size (Alamitta) | Closed Season (Rauhoitus) | Conservation Status & Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ahven** European Perch *Perca fluviatilis* | Ubiquitous in lakes, rivers, and Baltic brackish bays | **No statutory minimum** (Recommended: keep 20–30 cm) | None | National fish of Finland. Extremely abundant; release trophy specimens (>35 cm). |
| **Kuha** Zander / Pikeperch *Sander lucioperca* | Murky, warm lakes, sheltered coastal bays, Gulf of Finland | **42 cm** (some waters set 45 cm) | Local spawning bans apply in coastal bays (May–June) | Highly prized table fish. Undersized fish must be immediately released alive. |
| **Hauki** Northern Pike *Esox lucius* | Weed beds, reed edges, shallow bays across the entire country | **No statutory minimum** (Slot: keep 50–75 cm) | None (avoid disturbing pre-spawn shallows in April) | Apex predator. Critical to release large breeding females (>80 cm / 4+ kg). |
| **Taimen** (Wild / Rasvaevällinen) Brown / Sea Trout with adipose fin | Sea coast, Baltic archipelago, southern flowing rivers | **Completely Protected** | **Year-Round Protection** in sea and south of 64°N | Wild trout with intact adipose fin must be unhooked and released immediately in the water. |
| **Taimen** (Stocked / Rasvaeväleikattu) Fin-clipped Brown Trout | Managed rivers, coastal stocking zones | **50 cm** | 1 Sept – 30 Nov in flowing waters | Only hatchery-raised trout whose adipose fin was clipped can be legally retained. |
| **Siika** European Whitefish *Coregonus lavaretus* | Sandy seabeds, clear boreal lakes | **30–35 cm** (in sea areas) | Autumn river spawning bans (1 Sept – 30 Nov) | Targeted in early spring right after ice-out using earthworms on sandy bottoms. |
| **Silakka** Baltic Herring *Clupea harengus membras* | Baltic coastal waters and straits | **None** | None | Migrates in huge shoals past bridges in May–June and Oct–Nov. Completely free under general rights. |
| **Made** Burbot *Lota lota* | Deep cold lakes and brackish winter bays | **None** (Recommended: >40 cm) | None | Only freshwater gadid (cod relative). Caught at night through ice in Jan–Feb during spawning. |

        

        
## 🔍 How to Check the Adipose Fin (Rasvaevä) on Trout

        
When you land any trout in Finnish waters, your very first action before measuring length is to inspect the small fleshy fin located on the dorsal ridge between the main dorsal fin and the tail:

        
          - **Adipose Fin Present (Intact)**: This is a **wild trout** (*luonnontaimen*). In all Finnish maritime waters and anywhere south of 64°N latitude, wild trout are **strictly protected year-round**. Keep the fish in the water, gently remove the hook with long forceps, and release it immediately.
          - **Adipose Fin Missing (Neat scar)**: This is a **hatchery-stocked fish** (*istukas*) clipped as a juvenile. If it meets the statutory minimum length of **50 cm**, you may legally keep it.
        

        
> [!WARNING]
> Statutory Value of Protected Fish: Illegally killing an adipose-intact wild sea trout carries a mandatory compensatory conservation fine of €3,260 per fish under Finnish criminal law, plus revocation of fishing privileges.

## 🔍 关键实操：如何鉴别鳟鱼的“脂鳍（Rasvaevä）”

        
在芬兰水域（特别是波罗的海与南部水系）钓获任何鳟鱼时，起竿第一件事**绝不是量长度，而是立刻查看脂鳍**（位于背鳍后方、尾鳍前方背脊上的肉质小鳍）：

        
          - **脂鳍完整存在**：属于**野生海鳟/野生褐鳟**。在芬兰全部海域及北纬64度以南内陆水域，**全年全境绝对禁捕**！必须在水下直接用摘钩钳卸钩并放归，不得离水摔在碎石上。
          - **脂鳍位置缺失（平整切口愈合痕迹）**：属于**人工增殖放流鱼**。若体长测量达到或超过 **50 cm**，方可合法保留。
        

        
> [!WARNING]
> 违法代价极高： 依据芬兰法律，非法私藏或杀害一条野生完整脂鳍海鳟，除面临刑事扣押捕钓装备外，必须依法补缴每条 3,260 欧元的法定生态惩罚补偿金。

## Suomeksi (Finnish Version)

## 🐟 Kohdekalat, alamitat ja rauhoitusajat

        
Alamittojen ja rauhoitusaikojen noudattaminen on Suomessa kalastuslain ehdoton vaatimus. Rauhoitetun tai alamittaisen kalan ottamisesta seuraa kiinteä lakisääteinen korvausarvo valtiolle.

        
          
| Laji | Elinympäristö | Alamitta | Rauhoitusaika | Huomiot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ahven** | Järvet, joet, koko rannikko | **Ei lakisääteistä alamittaa** | Ei rauhoitusta | Kansalliskala, erinomainen ruokakala. Suositus: vapauta isot mätikalat (>35 cm). |
| **Kuha** | Tummat järvet, rannikon lahtivedet | **42 cm** | Paikallisia kuturauhoituksia touko-kesäkuussa | Tärkeä saaliskala. Alamittainen kuha on vapautettava välittömästi. |
| **Hauki** | Kaislikot, karikot, kaikki vedet | **Ei alamittaa** (Välikoko: 50–75 cm) | Ei yleistä rauhoitusta | Huippupeto. Yli 80 cm isot hauet on syytä vapauttaa vesistön tasapainon säilyttämiseksi. |
| **Taimen (rasvaevällinen)** | Meri, virtavedet | **Kokonaan rauhoitettu** | **Ympärivuotisesti rauhoitettu** meressä ja 64°N eteläpuolella | Luonnonvarainen taimen on vapautettava poikkeuksetta välittömästi vedessä. |
| **Taimen (rasvaeväleikattu)** | Istutusvedet | **50 cm** | 1.9.–30.11. virtavesissä | Vain eväleikatun kalan saa ottaa saaliiksi alamitan täytyttyä. |

        

        
> [!WARNING]
> Rasvaevällisen taimenen korvausarvo: Merialueella rauhoitetun luonnontaimenen laittomasta tappamisesta määrättävä valtiollinen korvausarvo on 3 260 € / kala.
